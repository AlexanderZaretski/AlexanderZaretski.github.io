06 - Method

How I Work With Data

I build systems that help people make decisions - not just dashboards.

01

Understand the decision being made

Every project starts with the decision it needs to support - not with the data that happens to exist.

02

Define the metric with stakeholders

A vague KPI gives a misleading answer. The definition is agreed with stakeholders before anything gets built.

03

Validate data quality and edge cases

Source, logic, and edge cases are checked first - a system built on bad data fails quietly.

04

Build clean, reusable pipelines

Readable SQL and Python that the next person can maintain and other teams can build on.

05

Deliver insights, not just charts

Every output ends with what the data means and what to do next.

06

Automate anything that repeats

Recurring questions become dashboards, scheduled reports, or alerts - and stop costing analyst time.

Problem

A big opening gap can trigger thousands of stop-loss and take-profit orders at once - moving client P&L and the company's hedge book - while a single hot stock can flood the pre-open order queue. The desk couldn't see any of it in time.

What I built

SQL detection in Databricks scanning every pre-market order against a 30-day baseline, plus hedging-exposure and queue-load checks, with automated Google Chat alerts to the operations team.

Result

The desk reviewed flagged stocks and adjusted hedges and limits before the open - risk was handled before it became a loss, not after.
